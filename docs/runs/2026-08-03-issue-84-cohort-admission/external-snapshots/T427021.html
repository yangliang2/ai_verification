Description

Steps to replicate the issue (include links if applicable):

  • Add two languages in the app. English and Russian
  • Observe community feed in English.
  • Switch your Community feed to Russian.
  • Observe the community feed is in Russian.
  • Switch over to the For You feed.

What happens?:

  • Language switcher is labeled Russian, but your "For you" feed is still in English. This could be confusing to users.

What should have happened instead?:

  • The language of the feed should always match the selector's current state

[Preferred] Behavior 1

  • The language switcher is universal, and when someone switches to the other view, the feed should also be in that language.
  • When someone updates their language from the language switcher, it should automatically update both views of the feed.

Or Behavior 2

  • The language switcher is a per-feed section setting, and it always defaults to the primary on each of the views unless the user updates it on that feed
