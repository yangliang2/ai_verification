Description

Steps to reproduce

  1. In the Wikipedia app for android configure multiple languages in Settings -> Wikipedia-languages
  2. Open some page in English Wikipedia
  3. 1. Then leave the tab open and search for that article in the app in some other language (e.g. Spanish)
  4. 2. Then close the tab and search for that article in the app in some other language (e.g. Spanish)

Expected results

When another language is selected in the search it should show the result for that language. For example one may want to open the same article (that is same subject) in two languages in two tabs.

Actual results

In the first image the English WP version of the article is open in another tab; in the second image I have closed the tab but it's still in the tab history. However, I wanted to see the Spanish Wikipedia article and could not open it. Notice that except for this one page all the other search results are from Spanish Wikipedia. Not even closing the tab did help but that shouldn't be required. If you think it's useful show both the Spanish and the English article but in the "Spanish" tab of the search I think only Spanish-WP pages should be shown (maybe at some point also e.g. ENWP article machine translated to Spanish in addition but still only Spanish pages).
